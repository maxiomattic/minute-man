Jon Rosenthal (D), Bo French (R), and Arthur DiBianca (L) ran in the general election for an at-large seat on the Texas Railroad Commission on Nov. 3, 2026. French defeated incumbent Commissioner James Wright (R), who was elected in 2020, in the Republican primary runoff 50.5% to 49.5%.

The Texas Railroad Commission is a state executive agency in Texas. Despite its name, the commission does not regulate railroads. Instead, it regulates the oil and gas business in Texas. Ahead of the 2026 general elections, all three commissioners were Republicans. The last time a Democrat won a general election for an at-large seat on the commission was 1990.[1]

The general election happened against the backdrop of a post that French made on social media on Sept. 13, 2026. French posted a photo of University of Texas students at a football game who appeared to be predominantly South Asian. He captioned it, "I heard UT graduation this year looked like this. I didn't believe it. The problem is now obviously far worse than anyone imagined."[2][3]

In response, Texas Democrats and U.S. Senators Ted Cruz (R-Texas) and John Cornyn (R-Texas), along with Texas State House Speaker Dustin Burrows (R), criticized French's comments. French defended making the post.[4][5] Click here to read more about the post.

The Dallas Morning News' Joseph Morton wrote that as a result of the post, "Texas Democrats are feeling upbeat about their chances in the midterms after 30 years without winning a statewide race, while Republicans are counting on a unified front to help them maintain control."[6]

Rosenthal was first elected to represent District 135 in the Texas House of Representatives in 2018. He previously worked as a project manager, an engineering manager, and a subsea systems engineer in the oil and gas industry for 25 years.[7]

In his Candidate Connection survey, Rosenthal said, "I'm running to restore integrity, accountability, and common sense. As a career oilfield engineer, a proven legislator, and a father, I will make sure the Railroad Commission works for the PEOPLE of Texas – not just wealthy special interests and political insiders."

Republican political strategist Karl Rove, AFL-CIO Texas, and the Houston Chronicle endorsed Rosenthal.[8][9][10]

French started working as an energy trader in 1994. He also owned businesses that provided security for energy companies in the Middle East. French served as the chair of the Tarrant County Republican Party from 2023 to 2025.[11]

In his Candidate Connection survey, French said, "As your next Railroad Commissioner, I will revolutionize the permitting process to help the industry and Texas thrive, end rampant DEI spending in the Commission, and get radical Islamists out of Texas energy."

President Donald Trump (R), the Texas Alliance of Energy Producers, and the Texas Oil and Gas Association endorsed French.[12][13][14]

Jon Rosenthal (D), Arthur DiBianca (L), and Bo French (R) completed Ballotpedia's Candidate Connection survey. To read those survey responses, click here.

Jon Rosenthal (D) won election in the general election for Texas Railroad Commission on November 3, 2026.
